Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how English Trainer uses cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies? Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites. They are often used to remember preferences and help measure how a site is used.

Preferences: English Trainer uses browser local storage to remember settings like theme, last selected context, and playback speed.

Advertising cookies: If ads are shown (for example, through Google AdSense), third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies to personalize and measure ads. You can manage cookie settings in your browser and, where available, via the ad provider’s controls.
