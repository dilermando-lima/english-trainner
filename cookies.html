This Cookie Policy explains how English Trainer uses cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies? Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites. They are often used to remember preferences and help measure how a site is used.

Preferences: English Trainer uses browser local storage to remember settings like theme, last selected context, and playback speed.

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense): This site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies and use similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and personalize advertising. These cookies are subject to Google’s Advertising and Privacy policy. You can manage or opt out of personalized ads in your browser and via Google Ad Settings.

Consent (EEA and UK): If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent for non-essential cookies (including advertising cookies) via the cookie notice shown when you first visit. You can change your mind by clearing site data or using your browser’s cookie settings. For full details, see our Política de privacidade.
