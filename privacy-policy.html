Política de privacidade

This Privacy Policy explains how English Trainer handles information when you use the app.

Personal data: English Trainer does not require you to create an account and does not ask for personal information to use the core features.

Local storage: The app stores a few preferences on your device (such as theme, last selected context, and speed) using your browser’s local storage so the experience stays consistent between visits.

Speech features: If you use pronunciation checking, your browser’s speech recognition feature may process audio to generate a transcript. This processing is handled by the browser and/or its provider.

Advertising (Google AdSense): This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, and personalize advertising. You can learn more about how Google uses data for advertising and how to control your preferences at Google’s Advertising and Privacy page. You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ad Settings.

Visitors in the EEA and UK: We use cookies and similar technologies for essential operation and preferences, and, when you consent, for advertising. You can accept or refuse non-essential cookies via the cookie notice on the site. Your choice is stored locally on your device. For more details, see our Política de cookies.

Contact: If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the Contato page.
