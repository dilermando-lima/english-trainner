Sobre

English Trainer is a free practice app for anyone who wants to improve their English with Portuguese translations. It is designed for learners who already have some basics and want to build vocabulary and listening skills in real-life situations.

You choose a context (for example greetings, work, travel, or daily routines), listen to sentences at a speed that suits you, and move step by step through questions and answers. You can also use the microphone to practice pronunciation and get feedback. All content is organized by everyday situations so you can learn phrases you will actually use.

The app runs in your browser and does not require an account. If you have questions or suggestions, contact me at dilo.silva.lima@gmail.com.
